The Horreum Spatial Pilot

The monumental granary/storehouse (91×27 m) serves as the primary spatial pilot: articulated internal layout with two longitudinal rows of supporting piers (3 aisles), western cella vinaria with 1.40 m deep masonry pools for wine and oil, southern covered porch with cart ramp, and embedded dolia defossa.

Published Architectural Dimensions Confirmed via primary monograph (Petrović 1994, SANU) and cross-verified secondary records.
London Charter Paradata Aisles and basin depths are documented; column grid remains schematic pending georeferenced plan digitization.

Delineation of Forensic Boundaries — What this Twin is Not

  • Not a speculative 3D render. We do not invent decorative roof tiles, marble capitals, or wall paintings without in situ evidence.
  • Octagon building explicitly excluded. Primary literature analysis (Gavrilović Vitas & Milošević Jevtić 2019, Starinar LXIX) confirmed that the published octagon belongs to the separate Gradsko polje site near Niš Fortress, not Mediana.
  • Schematic site arrangement. Building positions are relative/schematic until institutional permission for georeferenced cadastral plans is finalized.
  • Technology partner, not a competitor. Svemir provides open digital twin infrastructure to enhance Serbian academic heritage research and international European grant consortiums.
