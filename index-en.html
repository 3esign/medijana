Standards

Method and documentation

The pilot records data provenance and modelling decisions, using the London Charter (2009) and the Seville Principles (2011) as a methodological framework. The documentation distinguishes source data, analogies and hypotheses.

Method and standards (SR)→

Current pilot status
  • No new field data: this is a synthesis of published literature and public reports.
  • The scope of the next phase and access to field documentation will be agreed within the collaboration.
  • No HBIM yet — a schematic working model that functions as a spatial knowledge registry. The pilot with real survey plans is phase P2.
  • Neither the model layout nor the map overlay is georeferenced.
  • The bibliography is incomplete; every hypothesis is marked UNVERIFIED until tested.
Collaboration

Pilot with the Institute of Archaeology

The pilot provides a starting point for joint work on the digital documentation of Mediana. The next step is to agree on an initial building, available plans and a review process.

Use and publication of field material will be agreed with the responsible institutions and researchers.
